Interiors Buddy

Know what you're actually paying for

An interior/modular-furniture quote is a wall of line items in whatever format each vendor feels like using. This reads it for you, attaches a real ₹/sqft rate to every item, checks it against typical market ranges and your own floor plan, and tells you what's missing before you sign anything.

How this works

  1. Upload your floor plan (optional, but worth doing) — a photo or screenshot of the plan is enough. It lists every room it can find.
  2. Upload each vendor's quote as a PDF, or paste the text in if the PDF is a scanned image.
  3. Every line item gets read into a table with a ₹/sqft rate worked out automatically, from the stated rate or from the panel's height × width.
  4. Each rate is checked against a typical market range, each item against your own must-have list, and — if you uploaded one — each room against your floor plan, so an unpriced bedroom or a kitchen missing its overhead unit shows up on its own.
  5. Load two or three vendors to see them side by side, then ask for a plain-English read on the real gaps and which way to lean.
What should these quotes include?

One item per line. Every pasted quote is checked against this list, so anything missing shows up as a flag instead of something you have to notice yourself.

Guidance

Ask Claude to read everything entered so far — every vendor, every flag, the floor plan — and call out the real strengths, weak spots, and which way to lean.

Benchmark ranges and the AI reading of each quote are starting points, not verified facts — vendor terminology, your city, and material grade all move real prices. A ₹/sqft rate for an item quoted only by height × width is derived as (height mm × width mm ÷ 92903) × qty; running-feet, per-piece and lump-sum items are shown as entered since a sqft rate isn't meaningful for them. Quotes and floor plans you upload are sent to Anthropic's API to be read and are not stored by this app.
